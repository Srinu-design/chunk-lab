Pebble Store Help Centre: Returns and Refunds

Shipping
Orders ship within two business days from our warehouse in Coimbatore.  You can track your parcel from the Orders page once it leaves the ware-
house. Delivery usually takes three to five days.

Returns
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery in their original packaging. Damaged items can be returned within 60 days if you up-
load a photo of the damage.    To start a return, open the Orders page and choose Return item.

Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within seven working days of the return being approved. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to your bank account instead. Gift cards and downloadable products cannot be returned or refunded.

Bulk orders
For orders above fifty units, contact the sales team for a custom quote and delivery schedule.  Bulk orders follow the same return rules, but refunds may take up to four-
teen days.
